From ChatGPT to Telegram

The plugin gives you an HTML code block with an Open HTML to Rich link underneath.

If ChatGPT offers a rendered preview, copy the formatted message from that preview and paste it into Telegram’s rich-text editor.

If the preview is unavailable or the formatting is lost, paste the HTML source into the converter here. Check the preview, choose Copy Rich Text, then paste into Telegram.

Privacy

In ChatGPT. OpenAI processes your conversation according to your account settings. The plugin has no publisher-operated server that receives your drafts. You decide what to copy to Telegram. OpenAI privacy policy

In the converter. Your pasted HTML stays in this tab’s memory. The converter has no accounts, analytics or draft uploads. Clearing the source clears the preview. Your browser and device manage any clipboard copies.

Website hosting. GitHub Pages serves the site’s files and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The offline cache holds site files. It does not store your drafts. GitHub Pages data collection

Support email. Google processes support emails through Gmail. To respond, steffko uses your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Once your request is resolved, steffko deletes the support email thread. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email steffko. Google privacy policy
